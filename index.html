AI Training Privacy Policy

This application is for personal use only.

It accesses WHOOP data including workouts, recovery, sleep, strain, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, and body measurements for personal training analysis and planning.

Data is not sold or shared with advertisers.

Data may be stored in the app owner's private Google account for personal analysis.

WHOOP access can be revoked at any time through the user's WHOOP account.
